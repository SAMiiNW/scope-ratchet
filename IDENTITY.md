# Identity card: Scope Ratchet

- Mechanism signature: sequential change orders share one irreversible cumulative materiality counter.
- Product metaphor: an industrial torque gauge that advances but never rolls backward.
- Roles: buyer freezes the award; contractor files changes; oversight alone may correct a measurement during review.
- Voice: terse procurement inspector annotations.
- Evidence story: rulebook and award are digest-frozen; each order and challenge keeps its own source digest.
- Non-overlap: unlike policy preflight, appeals, or release quarantine, the defining decision depends on the sum of previously approved orders and an anti-splitting threshold.
